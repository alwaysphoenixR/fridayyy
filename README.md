## 📖 Executive Summary & Problem Statement

**Friday** is a high-performance Personal Knowledge Management (PKM) platform that acts as a secure, AI-powered "Second Brain." It allows users to ingest, organize, and semantically query diverse digital assets—spanning raw notes, web articles, tweets, and full PDF documents. 

**The Problem:** Our digital lives are fragmented across dozens of siloed applications, making it nearly impossible to retrieve specific knowledge when needed. Furthermore, while foundational LLMs offer immense reasoning capabilities, they lack personal context and are highly prone to hallucination. Friday solves this by providing a unified, multi-modal ingestion pipeline coupled with a sophisticated Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) engine, securely grounding state-of-the-art inference in the user's private dataset.
